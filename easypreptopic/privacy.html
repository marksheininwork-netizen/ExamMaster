Privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

EasyPrepTopic is an independent study prototype. This notice describes the current version of the EasyPrepTopic page inside ExamMaster.

Progress stored in your browser

The app saves only aggregate question counts—attempted and correct—in this browser’s local storage. It does not save your selected answers, name, school, or account information. Timed-mock answers stay in memory while the mock is open; submitting the mock updates the aggregate counts.

Progress is stored on this device and browser and does not sync. Clear site data for the ExamMaster site in your browser settings to remove the saved counts.

Network and hosting

The EasyPrepTopic page runs as a static app. Its code does not send answers or progress to an app server and does not include account, analytics, advertising, or payment features. The website host may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP addresses and server logs, under its own policies.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact marksheininwork@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, student records, or other sensitive information.
